Send a few real prompts

The reading test works best on real messages. Please add 5 to 10 things you asked an AI recently, in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or anything else.

What works

Messages that make sense on their own, like the first message of a chat. Everyday things are perfect: a question, an email or text to draft, advice, an explanation, a plan, feedback on something short.

Skip anything that needs a file or a long paste to make sense.

What happens to them

Jay sends them to several AI models (from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI and Microsoft) through GitHub Copilot, to get replies with and without the skill. You'll rate some of those replies later.

They stay private. Only totals get published, unless you tick "OK to publish" for a prompt, which lets that prompt and its replies appear in the public test data.

Leave out anything confidential: work secrets, customer details, or anyone's personal information. Rewording a prompt to remove details is fine.
